// Cultural Philosophy

Preserving Authentic Bahamian Rhythms

Bigga Tea (Trevor Taylor) and the collective Muh Buiy Dem Entertainment stand as guardians of true Bahamian sound. In a music landscape often diluted by external influences, Bigga Tea champions the unadulterated use of authentic dialect, cowbells, goat-skin drums, and rake 'n' scrape.

"Junkanoo is almost two hundred years old, and in a time when the world is looking for real roots and culture, we must celebrate our own." — Bigga Tea
