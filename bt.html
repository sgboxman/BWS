01 / The story

A sound with somewhere to come from.

Bigga Tea has long advocated for music that feels unmistakably Bahamian. In a 2012 Tribune profile, he described Muy Buiy Dem's sound as deliberately rooted in local dialect and indigenous instruments, including rake 'n' scrape, cowbells and drums. The group came together in 1995 and was later registered as an international music publishing company and record label.

“Regatta Time” was described as a true Bahamian regatta experience — mailboats, island hospitality and the excitement of sloop racing.

03 / Culture

Junkanoo, dialect, rhythm.

The heart of the story is cultural specificity. The 2012 Tribune piece describes Muy Buiy Dem's approach as an effort to keep Bahamian musical identity present year-round, using indigenous sounds and Bahamian dialect rather than simply imitating outside styles.
