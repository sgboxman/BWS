Welcome to Seafood Genie

Bringing the Finest Bahamian Catch Directly to Your Kitchen

Located conveniently on Fox Hill Road in Nassau, Seafood Genie is your local neighborhood seafood market. We take pride in sourcing high-quality, wild-caught seafood directly from local fishermen across the Bahamian islands.

Whether you're preparing a traditional Sunday dinner, a backyard fish fry, or searching for fresh local conch, our friendly team is here to provide you with expertly cleaned, cut, and prepared seafood every single day.
